THE CRIMSON ARCHIVE — Overlord-inspired personal website

FILES: index.html, style.css, script.js, my-photo.svg, README.txt.

The ZIP now includes my-photo.svg so the portrait area is never empty. This is a temporary fantasy-style placeholder, NOT a picture of you. To show your actual face, put your own photo in this folder and change the image src in index.html from my-photo.svg to your exact filename, for example my-photo.jpg.

RUN: Extract the ZIP and open index.html in your browser. Click each relic card to open its record. Press Escape or click outside a record to close it. Use the top-right sun icon to switch themes.

The site uses Cebu City, Philippines and does not display a street address. Google Fonts require internet access. The gothic dark-fantasy design is inspired by Overlord's atmosphere but uses original CSS artwork rather than copied anime images.
